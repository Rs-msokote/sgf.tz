Who We Are

Smile Global Foundation (SGF) is a registered Tanzanian non-governmental organization committed to creating positive and sustainable change in communities across Mainland Tanzania.

We work with communities, young people, women and girls, students, government institutions, and development partners to promote education, environmental conservation, empowerment, public health, gender equality, entrepreneurship, and sustainable community development.

Through partnerships and community-led initiatives, we empower people, protect the environment, and create opportunities for stronger and more resilient communities.

Area of Operation: Tanzania Mainland

Our Vision

SGF envisions urban and rural communities in Tanzania to have improved livelihood and maintained sustainable conservation of natural resources in the mainland especially empowering women.

Our Mission

To support communities to fight against poverty and promote good health through environmental guardianship, by building competences, quality education, entrepreneurship, sustainable management of natural resources, gender mainstreaming, and provision of social services.

Discover our latest initiatives and community response stories in action.

Environmental Conservation & Reforestation

April 2024, Smile Global Foundation (SGF) has partnered with FutureSense Foundation Tanzania by donating and transporting 15,000+ tree seedlings to support environmental conservation. The Tree Planting initiative has recorded an 85.9% tree survival rate, demonstrating the importance of continued environmental action, tree care, and community participation.

"Continued environmental action, tree care, and active community participation are essential for long-term sustainability."

Supporting Students After Enduimet Dormitory Fire

Following the fire that destroyed two girls' dormitories at Enduimet Secondary School in Longido District, Arusha Region, 26 August 2024, SGF supported affected students with essential items..

The support included mattresses and 50 blankets to help students continue their education in a safer and more supportive environment.

"No student should be left behind because of circumstances beyond their control."

Advancing Menstrual Health and Hygiene

Smile Global Foundation was proud to participate in the Salama Marathon 2024, supporting efforts to promote menstrual health and hygiene for girls and women in Tanzania. We conducted a media tour to raise awareness and encourage public participation.

We also joined the marathon, turning awareness into action and standing together with communities to promote safe and healthy menstrual practices.
