ABOUT US

Who We Are

Smile Global Foundation (SGF) is a Non-Governmental Organization registered under the Non-Governmental Organizations Act, No. 24 of 2002 as amended.

SGF operates at a National level across Tanzania Mainland, working exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes. We work with communities, students, youth, women and girls, and development partners to address social, environmental, and economic challenges.

Area of Operation: National (Tanzanian Mainland)

Our Vision

SGF envisions urban and rural communities in Tanzania to have improved livelihood and maintained sustainable conservation of natural resources in the mainland especially empowering women.

Our Mission

To support communities to fight against poverty and promote good health through environmental guardianship, by building competences, quality education, entrepreneurship, sustainable management of natural resources, gender mainstreaming, and provision of social services.

WHAT WE DO

Environment & Climate Action

We promote environmental protection and sustainable natural resource management through tree planting, climate awareness, community participation, and partnerships.

Together, we grow a greener future.

Women & Girls Empowerment

We empower women and girls through education, skills development, economic opportunities, menstrual hygiene awareness, and gender equality initiatives.

Empowered women build stronger communities.

Community Development

We work with local stakeholders to address poverty, improve livelihoods, protect children's rights, and build practical solutions to local challenges.

Practical change driven by communities.

IMPACT IN NUMBERS

See how SGF turns dedication into measurable community transformation.

15,000+
Environmental Conservation

Since April 2024, Smile Global Foundation (SGF) has partnered with FutureSense Foundation Tanzania by donating and transporting 15,000+ tree seedlings to support environmental conservation.

The Tree Planting initiative has recorded an 85.9% tree survival rate, demonstrating the importance of continued environmental action, tree care, and community participation.

100+
Supporting Girls' Education

Following the fire that destroyed two girls' dormitories at Enduimet Secondary School in Longido District, Arusha Region, on 26 August 2024, SGF supported affected students with essential items.

The support included 50 mattresses and 50 blankets to help students continue their education in a safer and more supportive environment.

2024
Advancing Menstrual Health & Hygiene

Smile Global Foundation was proud to participate in the Hedhi Salama Marathon 2024, supporting efforts to promote menstrual health and hygiene for girls and women in Tanzania. We conducted a media tour to raise awareness and encourage public participation.

We also joined the marathon, turning awareness into action and standing together with communities to promote safe and healthy menstrual practices.

Supporting Students After Enduimet Dormitory Fire

On 26 August 2024, two girls' dormitories at Enduimet Secondary School in Longido District were tragically destroyed by fire.

Led by Executive Director Tezra Semuguruka, SGF swiftly donated 50 mattresses and 50 blankets to ensure students could comfortably resume their education in safety.

"No student should be left behind because of circumstances beyond their control."
