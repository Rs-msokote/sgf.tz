About SGF

Smile Global Foundation (SGF) is a Non-Governmental Organization registered under the Non-Governmental Organizations Act, No. 24 of 2002 as amended from time to time.

SGF operates at a National level across Tanzanian Mainland, working exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes to eradicate poverty and promote sustainable community development.

Area of Operation: National (Tanzanian Mainland).

What We Do

ENVIRONMENT & CLIMATE ACTION

We promote environmental conservation, sustainable natural resource management, climate awareness, and community-led action to protect our environment for future generations.

WOMEN & GIRLS EMPOWERMENT

We empower women and girls through education, skills development, economic opportunities, gender awareness, and increased participation in community development.

COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT & ENGAGEMENT

We work with communities and local stakeholders to address poverty, improve livelihoods, promote health and well-being, protect children’s rights, and build sustainable solutions to local challenges.

News & Impact

Discover our latest initiatives and community support programs across Tanzania.

🌱 10,000+ Trees Supported for Planting

Smile Global Foundation, in partnership with Future Sense Foundation – Tanzania Hub, is committed to protecting the environment and promoting a greener and more sustainable future.

Since 2024, the partnership has supported tree-planting activities in schools and communities. By August 2026, more than 10,000 trees had been donated and supported for planting.

Through this initiative, we engage young people, students, schools, and communities in environmental conservation and encourage them to take practical action against environmental challenges.

"We believe that every tree planted today is an investment in a healthier environment and a better future for generations to come."

Supporting Students After Enduimet Dormitory Fire

On the night of 26 August 2024, two girls’ dormitories at Enduimet Secondary School in Longido District, Arusha Region, were destroyed by fire, affecting the living conditions of many students.

In response to this challenge, Smile Global Foundation, under the leadership of our CEO Tezra Semuguruka, donated 50 mattresses and 50 blankets to support the affected students.

The initiative was aimed at helping students recover from the loss and continue their studies in a safer and more comfortable environment.

"At Smile Global Foundation, we believe that no student should be left behind because of circumstances beyond their control."

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

SGF envisions urban and rural communities in Tanzania to have improved their livelihood and maintained sustainable conservation of natural resources in the Mainland, especially empowering women.

Our Mission

SGF's mission is to support communities to fight against poverty and promote good health through environmental guardianship, capacity building, quality education, entrepreneurship, sustainable management of natural resources, gender mainstreaming, and provision of social services.
