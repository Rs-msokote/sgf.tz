Who We Are

Smile Global Foundation (SGF) was registered in 2024 as a Tanzanian non-governmental organization committed to creating positive and sustainable change and improving the well-being of communities across Mainland Tanzania.

Area of Operation: Tanzania Mainland
26 Regions

Where We Work

SGF works across all 26 regions of Tanzania Mainland, addressing local needs and supporting inclusive and sustainable development.

Our Vision

SGF envisions urban and rural communities in Tanzania to have improved livelihood and maintained sustainable conservation of natural resources in the mainland especially empowering women.

Our Mission

To support communities to fight against poverty and promote good health through environmental guardianship, by building competences, quality education, entrepreneurship, sustainable management of natural resources, gender mainstreaming, and provision of social services.

What We Do

Our programs are designed to create lasting impact across communities.

Environment & Climate Action

We promote environmental protection and sustainable natural resource management through tree planting, climate awareness, community participation, and partnerships.

Together, we grow a greener future.

Women & Girls Empowerment

News & Stories

Discover our latest initiatives and community response stories in action.

Environment

Environmental Conservation

Since April 2024, Smile Global Foundation (SGF) has partnered with FutureSense Foundation Tanzania by donating and transporting 15,000+ tree seedlings to support environmental conservation. The Tree Planting initiative has recorded an 85.9% tree survival rate.

demonstrating the importance of continued environmental action, tree care, and community participation.

Supporting Girls' Education

Following the fire that destroyed two girls' dormitories at Enduimet Secondary School in Longido District, Arusha Region, 26 August 2024, SGF supported affected students with essential items.

The support included mattresses and 50 blankets to help students continue their education in a safer and more supportive environment.

Health & Hygiene

Advancing Menstrual Health and Hygiene

Smile Global Foundation was proud to participate in the Hedhi Salama Marathon 2024, supporting efforts to promote menstrual health and hygiene for girls and women in Tanzania. We conducted a media tour to raise awareness and encourage public participation.

We also joined the marathon, turning awareness into action and standing together with communities to promote safe and healthy menstrual practices.
